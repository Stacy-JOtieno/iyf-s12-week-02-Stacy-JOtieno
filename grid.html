Dangote IPO

The IPO—billed as "the people’s IPO"—raised about $4.78 billion to $4.8 billion in total orders, drawing nearly three to four times the original $1.6 billion base target. Because demand significantly outstripped supply, Dangote is expected to utilize its 30% over-allotment option, expanding the final fundraising scale to approximately $2.1 billion. The offering is currently going through verification and approval by the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC). Allotments are expected to be credited to Central Securities Clearing System (CSCS) accounts, with secondary market trading on the Nigerian Exchange (NGX) anticipated in late November or early December 2026.
